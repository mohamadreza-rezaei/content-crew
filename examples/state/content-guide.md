# Content guide (example)

Shared idea ~70%, brand execution ~30%.

## Brands
| Brand | Voice | Pillars | Audience | CTA | Theme | Connected platforms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Alex (personal)** | "I", practitioner | lessons, tools I tested, opinions | builders, peers | question to comments | emerald | LinkedIn |
| **Acme** (AI automation agency) | "we", solution-focused | automations, case patterns | SMB owners | book a call | sunset | Instagram, LinkedIn |
| **Forge Studio** (product studio) | "we", no AI hype | product thinking, validation | founders | DM us | violet | Instagram |

## Language rules
- Posts are in Persian. No line may start with a Latin word, URL, hashtag or mention (write "ابزار n8n …", not "n8n …").
- No em dashes. Hashtags after a label line, URLs after a label line.
